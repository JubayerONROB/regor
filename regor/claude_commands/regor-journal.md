# Journal preparation

Prepare for a journal the researcher has chosen.
1. `regor journal init "<Journal name>"`; fill journal_profile.yaml ONLY from the journal's official author guidelines and record the URL. Unknown fields stay null.
2. `regor journal check <slug>` and `regor journal export <slug> --format all`.
Rules: never claim acceptance or suitability; keep the scientific content independent of venue formatting.

Global rules (see CLAUDE.md): never fabricate results, citations or sources; never run paid, remote or long jobs without a researcher-granted approval; never edit runs/raw or runs/metadata.
